# Emmanuel Growth — BRIEF.md

> **Purpose:** This file defines what the Emmanuel Growth one-page website must communicate, who it is for, what it sells and what action the visitor should take.
>
> `DESIGN.md` defines how the website looks. This file defines what the website must accomplish.

---

## 1. Business

**Brand:** Emmanuel Growth

**Business type:** Marketing operations and growth systems.

**Core idea:**

Emmanuel Growth helps businesses:

1. get found
2. convert more enquiries
3. retain customers and build trust

The business uses practical tools such as websites, Google presence, AI-assisted conversations, lead capture, follow-up and review systems.

The customer does not need to understand the software behind the system.

---

## 2. Simple positioning

Primary positioning:

> **We help businesses get found, respond to leads faster and get more reviews.**

Expanded explanation:

> We use websites, Google, AI-assisted conversations and simple follow-up systems to help businesses turn more enquiries into customers.

This is not a software company.

This is not a chatbot company.

This is not primarily an AI creative studio.

Emmanuel Growth is a marketing operations business that solves practical customer-growth problems.

---

## 3. Website goal

A visitor should understand what Emmanuel Growth does within approximately 10 seconds.

The website must make it easy to answer:

- What does Emmanuel Growth help with?
- Which problem should I start with?
- What happens if I contact them?
- Why should I trust the approach?
- How do I get started?

The website should move a suitable visitor toward a conversation.

---

## 4. Primary audience

V1 should be useful to small and growing businesses that depend on enquiries, calls, messages, bookings, local discovery, repeat customers or reviews.

Examples may include service businesses and other businesses that receive customer enquiries through social media, WhatsApp, a website, Google or direct messages.

Do not lock the website to plumbers, electricians or one trade category.

The business may later create market-specific landing pages for selected niches or countries.

---

## 5. Current go-to-market emphasis

The **AI Lead Concierge** is currently the strongest front-door offer and may receive more visual and sales emphasis.

Reason:

Many businesses generate attention but lose potential customers because messages are:

- answered late
- missed
- forgotten
- scattered across different channels
- not followed up

This is especially relevant to markets where messaging and social enquiries are a major part of customer acquisition.

Important:

This emphasis does not replace the overall Emmanuel Growth model.

The full business remains:

**GET FOUND → CONVERT → RETAIN + BUILD TRUST**

---

## 6. Main offers

There are three main offers.

Each starts with a business problem, not with software.

---

### OFFER 1 — GET FOUND

**Service name:** Local Visibility & Lead Capture

**Problem**

People may already be looking for the business but cannot find it easily.

Or they find it and do not know what to do next.

**Solution**

Help the business become easier to discover and give potential customers a clear way to call, message, enquire or book.

**Services may include**

- Google Business Profile setup and improvement
- Google profile optimisation
- simple business website
- landing page
- contact forms
- WhatsApp button
- call button
- booking setup
- lead capture
- basic lead tracking

**Core website copy**

> **GET FOUND**
>
> Make it easier for customers to find you.
>
> We improve your online presence and give people a simple way to call, message or book.

**CTA:** Get Started

**Visual story**

Search / discovery  
→ business appears  
→ customer visits  
→ customer calls, messages or books

Do not imply guaranteed rankings or guaranteed lead volume.

---

### OFFER 2 — CONVERT MORE ENQUIRIES

**Service name:** AI Lead Concierge

**Problem**

Businesses receive messages from places such as Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and their website.

Some messages are answered late.

Some are forgotten.

Some good enquiries disappear.

Conversations may also be spread across several places.

**Solution**

Help the business respond faster, collect useful customer details, organise enquiries and move suitable leads toward the next step.

**Services may include**

- AI-assisted replies
- website chat
- Instagram enquiries where supported
- Facebook enquiries where supported
- WhatsApp enquiries where supported
- customer detail capture
- lead qualification
- booking
- human handoff
- organised conversations
- customer contact database
- simple follow-up

**Core website copy**

> **CONVERT MORE ENQUIRIES**
>
> Do not let good leads disappear.
>
> We help you reply faster, collect customer details and move enquiries towards a booking or sale.

**CTA:** See How It Works / Get Started

**Visual story**

Customer sends message  
→ fast assisted response  
→ details captured  
→ lead qualified  
→ booking, sale step or human handoff

**Important language rule**

Do not present AI as replacing the business owner or staff.

The story is:

**AI helps handle the repetitive first steps. People stay in control.**

Do not claim every messaging channel is supported in every setup. Use "where supported" when appropriate.

---

### OFFER 3 — RETAIN + BUILD TRUST

**Service name:** Reviews & Customer Follow-Up

**Problem**

Many businesses finish the job or sale and stop there.

They forget to ask satisfied customers for reviews.

They also lose opportunities to follow up with past customers.

**Solution**

Help the business ask for reviews, respond to feedback, stay in touch and bring suitable past customers back.

**Services may include**

- review requests
- Google review follow-up
- AI-assisted review reply support
- customer follow-up
- old-customer reactivation
- repeat-customer campaigns
- simple email follow-up
- simple SMS or messaging follow-up where appropriate
- customer list organisation

**Core website copy**

> **GET MORE REVIEWS. STAY IN TOUCH.**
>
> Turn happy customers into trust.
>
> We help you ask for reviews, reply to customers and stay connected after the sale.

**CTA:** Get Started

**Visual story**

Customer served  
→ review request  
→ customer leaves feedback  
→ business replies  
→ future follow-up  
→ repeat opportunity

Do not promise positive reviews. The system asks for honest customer feedback.

---

- Brunnet
- AI commercials
- AI creative production
- cinematic advertising
- Remotion
- video-generation tools

These capabilities can remain part of GrowthOS internally and may return later.

**GET FOUND**

1. **A customer sends a message**
2. **They get a fast first response**
3. **Useful details are collected**
4. **The enquiry is organised and qualified**
5. **The customer can book or a person takes over**

Use simple language.

A future demo can be placed here.

This section should sell the *business outcome*, not the technology.

### 9. Proof / examples

Until real client proof exists, this section may use:

- a working AI conversation demo
- a real lead-flow demonstration
- a review/follow-up demonstration

Label demos honestly.

If credible proof is not ready, keep this section minimal rather than filling it with placeholders that weaken trust.

**Heading**

## 9. Conversion behavior

The site should not force every visitor into the same long form.

Recommended V1 path:

**Get Started**  
→ short conversation/form  
→ identify main problem  
→ capture essential contact details  
→ human follow-up / booking

Useful first question:

> What would you like to improve?

Possible choices:

- Get found online
- Respond to enquiries faster
- Get more reviews
- Follow up with customers
- Not sure yet

Do not ask for unnecessary information before value is clear.

---

## 10. Voice rules

Use simple English.

Short sentences.

One idea at a time.

Speak about business problems in the language an owner would use.

### Prefer

- "Reply faster."
- "Do not let good leads disappear."
- "Make it easier for customers to find you."
- "Ask happy customers for reviews."
- "Stay in touch after the sale."

### Avoid

- "omnichannel orchestration"
- "AI-powered transformation"
- "revenue acceleration ecosystem"
- "lead lifecycle optimisation"
- "supercharge"
- "unlock growth"
- "leverage"
- unexplained technical terms

When a technical term is necessary, explain it immediately.

---

## 11. Truthfulness rules

Do not invent:

- client numbers
- revenue results
- response-time claims
- conversion-rate improvements
- testimonials
- customer logos
- awards
- locations served
- integration capabilities
- software partnerships

Do not claim a messaging channel works until the chosen implementation supports it.

Do not make guaranteed Google ranking claims.

Do not promise positive reviews.

Proof must come from real work, a real demo or clearly labelled concept/demo material.

---

## 12. Design relationship

`DESIGN.md` is the source of truth for:

- colors
- type
- spacing
- radius
- buttons
- section layout behavior
- responsive rules
- motion
- visual treatment

This brief must not introduce a competing design language.

The website should feel like Emmanuel Growth, not 9x.

The 9x reference is used for **system discipline**, not copied identity.

---

## 13. V1 completion standard

V1 is ready when:

- the offer is understandable within about 10 seconds
- the three offers are clear
- the AI Lead Concierge receives appropriate emphasis
- the site works well on mobile
- CTAs are obvious
- no fake proof exists
- no Brunnet or AI-commercial service section exists
- visual placeholders are deliberate, not random
- the site feels premium without becoming complicated
- the page can be deployed and shown to a prospect

Do not delay launch for decorative features that do not improve clarity, trust or conversion.
