# Redesign plan — GoHighLevel-style layout, Emmanuel Growth brand

Reference: gohighlevel.com (layout and rhythm only). We keep our own colours
(forest / ink / growth green), fonts (Oswald + Inter), copy and claims. No
borrowed numbers: proof strips use outcomes, not invented statistics.

## Phase 1 — Header + hero  ← current
- Dark, full-bleed hero band with a soft green glow (their deep-blue gradient, in our greens).
- Two columns: pill badge, big headline, one-line promise, primary CTA on the left;
  an illustrative "growth dashboard" panel on the right (built in HTML/CSS, not a screenshot).
- Dark header that sits on the hero, with a bright CTA button.
- Outcome strip under the hero (four plain-English outcomes, no figures).

## Phase 2 — "How it works" tabs
Their Capture / Nurture / Close / Evangelize / Reactivate tabs become
Get Found / Convert Enquiries / Build Trust / Follow Up, each tab showing a
checklist and a small UI panel.

## Phase 3 — Why us + proof
Three value pillars (their "All-in-one / AI / Community" row), one featured
testimonial card once we have a real one, and a simple packages section
(replaces their pricing cards; prices only if you supply them).

## Phase 4 — Closing + service pages
Big CTA band, richer footer, then carry the same components into
/get-found, /convert-enquiries and /build-trust. Mobile pass on every page.
